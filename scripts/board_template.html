Categories are a keyword screen, not a guarantee. A title can earn more than one badge — "Embedded Software Engineer Intern" gets both Software Engineering and Embedded Systems. See config/role_categories.yaml to retune the keyword lists. Review state is saved in this browser only.

Seasons come from the title first, then the source's own tag. Winter and Spring of the same year share one bucket. A posting can sit in more than one season (e.g. a Spring + Summer co-op); "Term not listed" means neither the title nor the source said.

"Notified" means the company is on your list, not that this exact posting pinged. Discord only fires for companies in config/notify_companies.yaml — everything else here still passed the location/internship/category filters, it just isn't one you asked to be pinged for. Rebuilt once a day, each morning around 7 AM ET, from the live feeds — so a posting that disappears here has been closed or filled upstream.

Day headings are the source's posting date, not the build date. A posting the source published no date for is filed under Undated rather than being dated to the day the page happened to be built. Undated does not mean old.
